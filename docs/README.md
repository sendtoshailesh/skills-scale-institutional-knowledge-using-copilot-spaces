# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation Hub

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation! This README serves as your central navigation point for understanding how we manage projects, from initiation through delivery and continuous improvement.

## Overview

OctoAcme approaches project management through a structured, iterative methodology that emphasizes clear goals, strong stakeholder alignment, regular communication, and continuous improvement. Projects are initiated with a focus on understanding the business need, defining measurable outcomes, and identifying key stakeholders. The planning phase breaks down work into manageable increments and defines dependencies, milestones, and risk strategies.

Execution is guided by disciplined workflows using project boards and frequent team touchpoints, such as daily standups and weekly syncs. Quality assurance is integrated throughout, leveraging automated testing, peer reviews, and defined acceptance criteria. Communication takes place on a regular cadence—through standups, stakeholder syncs, and written status updates—ensuring transparency, rapid escalation of blockers, and alignment across teams.

Roles are clearly delineated, from Product Managers driving vision and prioritization, to Project Managers facilitating delivery, and Developers executing the technical work. Supporting documentation such as risk registers and retrospectives foster a culture of learning and enable the team to adapt processes as needed. By maintaining living documentation and structured feedback loops, OctoAcme enables consistent, repeatable, and high-quality project delivery.

This README is your navigation starting point for all current project management best practices at OctoAcme. Please consult the linked documents below for specifics on each workflow, role, risk management approach, and continuous improvement practice—and contribute back as you identify opportunities to improve!
